About me

Hey! I’m Ashan, a Software Engineering undergraduate at NSBM Green University in Sri Lanka, and I love turning ideas into working apps. I mostly build for the web and mobile, and I’m happiest when a project goes from a rough sketch in Figma to something real people can tap, scroll and use.

Right now, I’m in my third year and I build things like responsive websites, cross-platform mobile apps, and the backends and databases that power them, from user logins and real-time data to REST APIs. Along the way, I use AI tools to move faster, while making sure I understand every piece of code I ship.

When I’m not studying, you’ll probably find me gaming, practicing Kandyan dancing, or coding a side project just for fun.
